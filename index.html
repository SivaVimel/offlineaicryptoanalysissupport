Getting started

  1. Open the app and accept the "Information, not advice" screen.
  2. Markets loads the top 500 coins live. Type a ticker such as SOL in the search bar to open a coin, BTC ETH to compare, or start with ? to ask the AI analyst.
  3. Tap the star on a coin to watch it, and ⋯ to add it to your portfolio, set an alert or compare it.

Live data

Prices, market caps, supply, volume, history and headlines come from Yahoo Finance's public endpoints, requested directly by your device; crypto quotes are sourced from CoinMarketCap. Each quote shows its time and can lag an individual exchange by seconds to minutes. Totals and dominance cover the coins loaded (top 250, 500 or 1,000 — Settings → Market data).

Prices refresh on the interval you choose (15 seconds to 5 minutes, default 30 seconds) while the app is open. Nothing refreshes in the background. If the connection drops, the last data stays on screen and the app keeps trying.

The AI analyst

The analyst answers questions about a coin, a comparison, the market, your portfolio or a term. Every figure comes from the app's own calculations. The on-device model (Llama 3.2 1B) only rewrites the wording, and a rewrite is thrown away if it contains a figure, a name or a reason not in the data, or any forecast or recommendation — the caption under each answer says which you are reading.

Generation is tied to what is on screen. Leaving a screen, switching section, opening a sheet or asking a new question stops whatever was being written, so the model never works in the background.

It won't predict prices or tell you to buy, sell or hold. That is deliberate.

By default everything runs on the device. If you prefer, Settings → AI engine → Custom endpoint lets you use a model server you run yourself, such as llama.cpp on your own network; on-device remains the default and the fallback.

Common questions

QuestionAnswer
How do I compare a coin with something other than bitcoin?On a coin's Performance tab, tap "vs BTC" and pick any coin. Long-press any coin, or use ⋯ → Compare with…, to open a chart of the two. Change the default in Settings → Compare coins with.
A coin shows no market cap.It is outside the loaded list, so it is priced from its own chart, which carries no supply data. Raise the list to the top 1,000 in Settings.
Why isn't the biggest gainer in Movers?Movers require at least $25M market cap and $1M daily volume and leave out stablecoins and wrapped tokens, so illiquid tokens don't dominate.
The summary says "Written by the app".The AI's version didn't pass the checks, the AI is off, or it is still loading. The figures are the same either way. Tap Rewrite to try again.
Can I see figures in bitcoin?Yes — choose BTC or ETH as the display currency on Markets or in Settings.
Do alerts work when the app is closed?No. Alerts are checked each time prices refresh while the app is open, and the app says so.
Does it connect to my exchange or wallet?No. Holdings are typed in by you and stay on your device.
How do I move my data to a new device?Settings → Export a backup, then Restore from a backup on the new device.
How do I delete everything?Settings → Erase all data, or delete the app.

Requirements

iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later. About 800 MB of storage, most of it the on-device AI model. Live prices need an internet connection; the last loaded data, your portfolio, the glossary and the AI keep working without one.
